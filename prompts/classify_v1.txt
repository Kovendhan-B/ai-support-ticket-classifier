- BILLING: Issues related to payments, invoices, refunds, or charges.
- TECHNICAL: Problems with product functionality, bugs, errors, or technical troubleshooting.
- ACCOUNT: Requests or issues regarding user accounts, login, password, or profile management.
- SHIPPING: Questions or problems about order delivery, tracking, or shipping status.
- GENERAL: Any other inquiries or requests not covered by the above categories.

- LOW: No immediate action required; informational or minor issues.
- MEDIUM: Needs attention but not urgent; moderate impact.

- POSITIVE: The user expresses satisfaction, gratitude, or positive feedback.
- NEUTRAL: The message is factual, with no clear positive or negative emotion.
- NEGATIVE: The user expresses dissatisfaction, disappointment, or negative feedback.
- FRUSTRATED: The user is clearly upset, angry, or repeatedly complaining.
